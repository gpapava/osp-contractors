Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible website

OSP Contractors wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

Conformance status

We have reviewed this website against the WCAG 2.1 level A and AA criteria and fixed the issues we found. We believe it meets the criteria apart from the limitations listed below, but this is our own assessment and has not yet been verified by an independent auditor. Until that verification is done, we describe the website as partially conformant.

What we have done

  • Text and controls have sufficient colour contrast, and the keyboard focus is always visible.
  • All functions can be used with the keyboard, and a "Skip to main content" link is provided.
  • Images have text alternatives, and pages use headings, lists and landmarks so that assistive technology can navigate them.
  • The site works at 200% zoom, on narrow screens (320 px) and with increased text spacing, without loss of content.
  • The website is available in Greek and English, and the language of each page is declared.
  • Moving content (the home page video and the photo carousel) can be paused, and respects the "reduce motion" setting of your device.
  • Form errors are described in text next to the field and in a summary at the top of the form.

Known limitations

  • Some PDF documents may not be fully accessible to screen reader users. For the ESPA poster and the Anti-Harassment & Whistleblowing Policy we provide accessible alternatives: ESPA poster (HTML) and Policy (HTML).
  • The interactive map on the Contact page is provided by a third party (OpenStreetMap / Leaflet) and may not be fully accessible. The addresses of our offices are also given as text.
  • The accessibility toolbar is provided by a third party, Skynet Technologies. It is an optional extra and does not replace the accessibility of the website itself.

How we assessed this website

This is a self-assessment. We ran automated checks (axe-core) on every page and checked keyboard use, zoom, text spacing and layout on small screens by hand. Testing with screen readers has been limited so far.

This statement was last reviewed on 17 November 2026.

Feedback and contact
